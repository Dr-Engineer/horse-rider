# Asset bible

Every asset is generated by a script in `art/`, so anything can be rebuilt or restyled. All files land in
`assets/`. Run through the Blender MCP with `tools/bmcp.py exec art/blender/<script>.py`.

## Characters: `art/blender/rider.py` → `assets/models/rider.glb`

One rig, and a wardrobe of skinned meshes in one file. The game shows the pieces each rider wears.

**Rig:** root, hips, spine, chest, neck, head, braid 1-2, arm_u/arm_l/hand/grip, thigh/shin/foot.
Weapons attach at `grip.R`; bow and shield at `grip.L`.

**Bodies** (slot A robe, slot B trim):
- `body_chuba`: bloused robe, sash, split skirt, diagonal front overlap, knee boots with banded uppers
- `body_oneoff`: chuba with one sleeve off the shoulder, showing a bright shirt
- `body_fleece`: long-sleeved fleece lokbar, wide trim, for Changtang and Amdo
- `body_hide`: hide robe hitched to bare knees
- `body_brocade`: stiff dragon-pattern court robe with a gold belt

**Heads:** `head_<face>` for young man, old man, young woman, older woman, soot-eyed. Skin tone per rider.

**Head pieces:** `hair_wound`, `hair_plaits`, `hair_mop`, `hair_short`, `hair_earlocks`, `hat_felt`,
`hat_big`, `hat_peak` (white brim, red tassels), `hat_fox`, `hat_basin`, `hat_disc`, `hat_foreign`.

**Extras:** `x_satchels`, `x_backpanel`, `x_casebell`, `x_bale`, `x_armcloth`, `x_scarf`.

**Animations (30 fps):** ride, ride_slow, ride_whip, wobble, swing_R/L, fist_R/L, thrust_R/L, kick_R/L,
bow_draw, bow_hold, bow_loose, block, throw, hit_R/L, tumble, lie, getup, run, mount, rest_sit, victory, stand.

## Horse: `art/blender/horse.py` → `assets/models/horse.glb`

Stocky Tibetan pony. Slot C coat, D mane and tail, B saddle rug.
Tack: paired rugs with medallion, wooden saddle with tall arches, girth, breast band with bells, red
throat tassel, bridle with brass band, poll plume, stirrups, crupper.

**Animations:** gallop, canter, trot, walk, idle, leap, rear, stumble.
Rider attaches to the `saddle` bone. No animation ever shows a horse hurt.

## Weapons: `art/blender/weapons.py` → `assets/models/weapons/*.glb`

Model along +Y from the grip at the origin.

- `sword`: straight single edge, trefoil pommel with coral bead, wire grip, cupped guard
- `knife`: short blade, bone handle
- `spear`: 3 m shaft with spiral iron banding, iron head, butt spike, yak tuft, silk streamers
- `roped_spear`: 1.5 m, loop of rope
- `key`: big iron key on a thong
- `whip`: braided, weighted head
- `pencase`: iron tube with cap
- `sling`: two cords with diamond cradle and tassels
- `bow` and `arrow`, `quiver`
- `shield`: round woven cane with a central boss

## World props: `art/blender/props.py` → `assets/models/props/*.glb`

**Sacred sites (indestructible, collision only):** `mani_wall` (long stone wall with abstract carved
marks), `chorten` (stepped base, dome, spire), `cairn` (lhatse with sticks), `flagpole` (tarchen), `flag_string`.

**Nomad:** `tent_black` (low black tent with guy ropes), `yak`, `sheep`, `dog` (mastiff with red collar),
`mule` (with pack load), `kiang`, `marmot`, `crane`, `dung_stack`, `churn`, `tea_brick_stack`, `wool_bale`.

**Plants and rock:** `spruce`, `juniper`, `willow`, `bush`, `grass_tuft`, `barley_patch`, `rock_a/b/c`,
`boulder`, `drying_rack`.

**Buildings:** `house_white` (Central Tibet: battered white walls, black windows, pema frieze), `house_kham`
(stone base with red log upper floor), `house_tsang` (grey with red and white stripes), `gate_arch`,
`bridge_cant` (cantilever bridge deck).

**Monuments:** `potala`, `chakpori_hill` (rock with medical college), `gate_chorten` (Western Gate over the
road), `dzong` (fort on rock), `kumbum` (nine tiers, gold top), `monastery`.

**Festival:** `crowd_a/b/c`, `food_booth`, `festival_tent` (white with blue trim), `start_gate`, `finish_gate`,
`banner`, `target_pole` (red cloth), `ramp`, `scarf`.

**Era:** `survey_stake`, `truck` (1950s lorry, parked, Season 3 only).

## Atlas tiles: `art/tools/atlas_tiles.py`

Faces (5), rug medallions (3), robe trim bands (4), brocade dragon-free pattern, wall with window, stripes,
flag tiles (5), wood grain, thatch and stone, mani stone marks, wool bale, tent weave.

## Road tiles: `assets/atlas/road_*.png`

One per course, small repeating pixel textures with ruts, stones and hoofprints. No symbols.

## Audio: `art/tools/make_audio.py` → `assets/audio/*.wav`

**SFX:** hooves at each gait (loops), wind (loop), hit_light, hit_heavy, clang, whoosh, bow_draw, bow_loose,
sling, rope, crash, grunt, bell, crowd (loop), horn, drum_hit, cymbal, flag_up, ui_move, ui_ok.
**Music:** `music_menu`, `music_race`, `music_final`, original loops in a pentatonic mode.

## UI: `assets/fonts/`, `assets/ui/`

Bitmap font, flag pips, weapon icons, rival portraits rendered from the real models by `tools/make_portraits`.
